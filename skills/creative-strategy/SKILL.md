## Finding the authentic story

The best Avalanche stories follow one shape: **serious institutions quietly doing something that used to be impossible.** Interrogate any brief with:

1. Who is the human? (a treasurer, a fan, a compliance officer, a founder — not "the ecosystem")
2. What could they not do on Monday that they can do now? (outcome, ideally with a number: seconds, cents, weeks-to-production)
3. Why is it credible? (named partner, regulated status, production usage — never hypotheticals)
4. Where's the tension? (legacy slowness vs. avalanche momentum — this is the brand's built-in drama)

If a story can't pass 1–3, it's not ready; say so and suggest what proof would unlock it.

## Concept generation

Deliver 3–5 distinct territories, each with: name, one-line idea, why it's ours (pillar fit), hero execution, and how it extends (social, OOH, film, event). Use brand devices: calm-amid-the-avalanche humor, snow/business juxtaposition, ALL-CAPS declaratives, digital blocks. For scripts, deliver premise + treatment paragraph + sample VO lines in manifesto cadence; for full video production, hand off to the `avalanche-video` plugin (`/make-video`) if installed.

## PR hooks

Strong hook types for Avalanche: first/biggest/regulated milestones ("first SEC-registered X"), counterintuitive numbers ("$2B moved for less than a coffee"), legacy-vs-now contrasts (T+2 vs. seconds), credible-messenger stories (bank exec, not crypto founder, tells it), data drops from network stats, and calendar-jacking finance moments (earnings seasons, payments conferences). Every hook needs: headline a journalist would actually write, target outlet tier (financial press first: FT/WSJ/Bloomberg/Axios; then trades: CoinDesk/The Block/Blockworks/Fortune Crypto), spokesperson, and proof asset.

## What to stay away from

- Price/token-performance narratives; anything resembling investment advice
- Meme-chasing that costs credibility (the brand test: would a bank CTO cringe?)
- Punching down at competitors by name in owned media — win on whitespace instead
- Overclaiming: "never," "guaranteed," "unhackable," unverified TPS wars
- Speculative partnerships or logos without approval; yield promises without compliance review
- Hype vocabulary (see avoid-list in `../../brand/copy-bank.md`)
