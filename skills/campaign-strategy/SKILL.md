# Campaign Strategy

Produce a decision-ready campaign brief through the Built for Business lens.

## Inputs to confirm (ask only what's missing)

Objective (awareness / pipeline / launch / ecosystem), audience (institutions, fintechs, enterprises, developers, community — see audience lenses), the announcement or asset at the center, timing/constraints, budget tier, and success definition.

## Grounding

- Messaging: `../../brand/messaging-framework-PRIVATE.md` — pick ONE pillar as the campaign spine; don't say everything at once.
- Facts/proof points: `../../brand/avalanche-knowledge.md`; verify anything technical against live docs (append `.md` to build.avax.network URLs).
- Look/feel: `../../brand/visual-guidelines.md` (Momentum + Elemental narratives; Avalanche IRL imagery; digital blocks).
- Competitive context: run a quick web scan of what competitors are doing in the same space so the campaign has a sharp differentiation angle.

## Brief structure

1. **The one-liner** — campaign idea in a sentence.
2. **Objective & KPI targets** (business outcomes first: SQLs, partner inquiries, developer signups — not just impressions).
3. **Audience & insight** — the audience lens plus the human tension the campaign resolves.
4. **Messaging architecture** — hero line (offer 3 options from /copywriting patterns), supporting proof points, CTA.
5. **Channel plan** — owned (site, blog, Snow Report, socials), earned (PR hooks — see /creative-strategy), paid (where the audience actually is: financial press, LinkedIn, OOH in financial districts, podcasts, event presence), events.
6. **Content calendar** — week-by-week with dependencies (tease → launch → proof → sustain).
7. **Creative direction** — visual treatment referencing the brand narratives; note formats (hero film, cutdowns, statics, endcards — see /creative-assets for templates).
8. **Risks & what we won't do** — avoid-list awareness, compliance flags, trend-hijack risks.
9. **Measurement plan** — per channel, with review cadence.

Deliver as a structured doc; offer local file, Google Doc (if their Drive connector is connected), or a slide-ready outline for the 2026 presentation template. If the `marketing:campaign-plan` skill is installed, it can generate a complementary generic calendar — this skill remains the brand source of truth.
