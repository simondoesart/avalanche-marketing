# Avalanche Social Audit

Honest self-audit of Avalanche-owned channels. Primary: X @avax. Secondary if requested: LinkedIn (avalancheavax), YouTube (Avalancheavax), Reddit r/Avax, Arena.

## Process

1. **Gather** recent posts (default lookback 30 days) via web search and browser tools: post topics, formats, cadence, engagement patterns, community replies/sentiment, and how launches (e.g. Payments Collective-type announcements) were covered. Note data limitations honestly. If the user can export analytics (X analytics CSV), ask for it — quantitative beats scraped.
2. **Evaluate against brand**, using `../../brand/messaging-framework-PRIVATE.md` and `../../brand/visual-guidelines.md`:
   - Voice check: are we outcome-led, precise, credible — or drifting into crypto-hype or corporate-bland?
   - Visual check: dark-mode, red-dominant, Aeonik-style consistency in cards/video?
   - Pillar balance: institutional proof vs. ecosystem/community vs. product/education vs. culture
   - Are we telling business stories (BlackRock, FIFA, payments) with any emotional pull, or just press-releasing?
3. **Score & recommend**:
   - Top 5 wins with the mechanism behind each (not just "high engagement")
   - Top 5 misses and root cause (topic, format, timing, hook)
   - Cadence/format recommendations; 5 ready-to-draft post concepts in our voice
   - One "brave bet" — a format we haven't tried that fits Momentum/Elemental brand energy
4. **Deliver** as a scorecard report; offer local file or Google Doc.

Constructive candor is the point: this is an internal tool — flag weaknesses plainly, and never inflate our numbers.
