# Tone of Voice

## Load the source

Read `../../brand/messaging-framework-PRIVATE.md` (relative to this skill's base directory). If missing (public-repo clone), fall back to the guardrails below plus `../../brand/copy-bank.md`.

## The voice in one line

Avalanche sounds like the most credible system in the room: confident, precise, measured — conviction over excitement.

## Guardrails (always apply)

1. Lead with outcomes, not abstractions ("Settlement in seconds" not "reimagining the future of finance").
2. Sound like infrastructure, not ideology.
3. Reliability and uptime over experimental novelty.
4. Revenue generation over vanity metrics.
5. Confident, precise, measured. Short declaratives. No hedging, no hype.
6. Never: degen slang, moon talk, price speculation, exclamation stacking, emoji-heavy institutional copy.

## Modes of use

- **"Show me the rules"** → summarize the guardrails, pillars, and audience lenses; offer the full framework details.
- **Review a draft** → score it against each guardrail, flag deviations with severity, and provide a rewritten version. Show before/after for the worst lines.
- **Rewrite/translate** → rewrite the user's copy in Avalanche voice; if an audience is named (enterprise, fintech, institutions, developers, community), apply the matching audience lens from the framework.
- **Channel calibration** → same truth, different temperature: X can be wrier and punchier; LinkedIn more institutional; blog/press most formal. The guardrails never bend — only sentence length and warmth do.

If the `brand-voice` or `marketing:brand-review` plugins are installed, they can run a deeper structured review — offer them for high-stakes content (press releases, exec comms).
