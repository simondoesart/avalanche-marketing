# Competitor Social Audit

Audit the social presence of one or more competitors (default set: Solana @solana, Ethereum @ethereum / EF accounts, Ripple @Ripple, Canton @CantonNetwork, Sui @SuiNetwork — confirm with the user).

## Process

1. **Gather.** For each account, use web search (and the Chrome browser tools if search results are thin) to find: recent posts from the last 30 days, formats used (text, threads, video, memes, infographics), posting cadence, campaign hashtags, notable spikes (viral posts, ratio moments), and follower/engagement signals where visible. X blocks logged-out scraping — prefer searches like `site:x.com solana`, coverage articles, and third-party analytics mentions; be transparent about data limits.
2. **Assess per account**:
   - Voice: how do they sound? (hype, technical, memetic, institutional)
   - Content pillars and mix; production quality vs. speed
   - What's working: formats/topics with outsized engagement, and WHY (hook, timing, cultural relevance)
   - What's not working: low-engagement patterns, tone misfires, community backlash
3. **Compare to Avalanche's lane.** Avalanche's voice is confident, precise, measured — the most credible system in the room (guardrails in `../../brand/messaging-framework-PRIVATE.md`). Identify: tactics worth adapting (translated into our voice, never copied), tactics to explicitly avoid, and gaps no one is filling (e.g. institutional storytelling that's actually entertaining).
4. **Deliver** a per-account scorecard plus a "steal / adapt / avoid" summary and 5 concrete post ideas for Avalanche inspired by the findings (drafted in our tone, using `../../brand/copy-bank.md`).

Offer output as local file or Google Doc (if the user's Google Drive connector is connected).
