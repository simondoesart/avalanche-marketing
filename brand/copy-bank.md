## Words & phrases we use

Production, infrastructure, settlement, finality, reliability, uptime, compliant, permissioned, sovereign, embedded finance, tokenized, real-world, at scale, deliberate, durable, credible.

## Words & phrases we avoid

To the moon, ape, degen, WAGMI (except the L1 name), 100x, moonshot, "revolutionary/disruptive" hype framing, "trustless" without explanation, "web3 magic," excessive exclamation points, emoji-heavy institutional copy, price/token speculation, "get rich," yield promises without compliance review, anything that sounds like ideology instead of infrastructure.

## Formula patterns

- **[Legacy pain] → [Avalanche outcome]**: "Two business days. Or two seconds. Your choice."
- **Calm authority juxtaposition**: pair an avalanche of change with an unfazed professional. "The market moved. So did the money."
- **Triads**: "Private. Compliant. Connected." / "Fast. Powerful. Secure." — three punchy words, full stops.
- **Manifesto cadence**: short declaratives, no hedging. "Ideas become infrastructure. Experiments become companies."
