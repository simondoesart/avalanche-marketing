# Avalanche Visual Guidelines (June 2026)

Source: Avalanche Brand Guidelines PDF, © Ava Labs 2026. Tagline: **Technology Built for Business**. Brand URL on creative: **avalanche.com** (site currently at avax.network; full migration to avalanche.com is planned — use avalanche.com on new brand creative, avax.network for live links until migration).

## Core idea

Avalanche exists to put the world's most powerful technology to use. Visually capture **the momentum of an avalanche** — a force of nature meeting the world of business.

## Typography

- **Headlines: Aeonik Black, ALL CAPS** — the primary headline voice (e.g. "TECHNOLOGY BUILT FOR BUSINESS").
- **Headline alt: Aeonik Regular** (all caps or sentence case for legibility).
- **Body: Aeonik Regular.**
- **Captions, numbers, data, eyebrows: Aeonik Fono** (monospaced feel; uppercase eyebrows at ~16pt).
- **Substitute when Aeonik is unavailable: Inter.**
- Reference scale: display headline Aeonik Black caps 64pt; subhead Aeonik Regular 36pt; small heading Aeonik Medium 24pt; body Aeonik Regular 16pt; eyebrow/CTA Aeonik Fono Medium 16pt.

## Visual narratives

1. **Momentum** — horizontal movement everywhere: horizontal layouts, horizontal red/gray blocks, vertical measurement (rule) lines for compartmentalization, motion blur imagery, easing curves and slide transitions in animation, "digital streams."
2. **Elemental** — an avalanche is a force of nature. Reference the natural world: snow, ice, frost, wind, drifts, cliffs, rocks, mountains. Audio of wind/ice/avalanches; visual and audio ASMR.

## Layout & composition

- 12-column grid, 5% margin, horizontal gutter 25% of margin, 6 rows, 1px vertical gutters.
- Clean, orderly grids for institutional use cases — break them deliberately for impact.
- Vertical rule lines add movement, compartmentalization, and visual interest.
- Maintain momentum: reveal horizontally in motion work.

## Imagery styles

1. **Avalanche IRL** (loudest level): avalanche snow taking over familiar business locations — offices, trading floors, filing cabinets — with people calmly unfazed. Conveys momentum + hidden technology powering real-world systems.
2. **Natural elements**: close-ups of snow, ice, wind, mountains; juxtapose natural vs. tech worlds.
3. **Motion blur**: cityscapes, ports, crowds, architecture with motion blur — momentum.
4. **Juxtaposition diptychs**: finance/tech imagery (payment card, NYSE facade, skyline) paired with powerful snow/avalanche imagery.
5. **Architectural sublime**: close-ups of architectural details and familiar finance environments — trust and institutional credibility.

## Graphic assets

- **Digital blocks pattern**: cascading horizontal red/gray blocks on the grid; they represent data, the blockchain, consensus, and a digitized avalanche.
- **Logo**: logomark (mountain "A") + logotype "avalanche" lowercase. Asset naming: `AVALANCHELOGO_HORIZONTAL_4C_KO`, `_1C_RED`, `_1C_GRAY`, `_1C_K`, vertical variants, `AVALANCHE_LOGOMARK_RED/WHITE/K`, `AVALANCHE_AVAXTOKEN` (red circle token mark), `POWEREDBYAVALANCHE_*` badges.
- Maintain safe space around the logo (logomark-width clearance); never crowd or distort.

## Quick do/don't

- DO lean dark mode, red-dominant, horizontal, ALL-CAPS Aeonik Black headlines.
- DO use snow/business juxtaposition and calm-amid-the-avalanche humor.
- DON'T use gradients-heavy, neon, meme-y crypto aesthetics; no rainbow palettes; no hype visual language. Avalanche should look like the most credible system in the room.
